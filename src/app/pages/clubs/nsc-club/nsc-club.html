Our Story

Discipline, Teamwork
and Leadership.

The Nalapad Sports Club (NSC) is a dedicated platform for students to discover their sporting potential, develop teamwork, and pursue excellence beyond the classroom. NSC brings together a wide range of sporting activities, regular competitions, training opportunities, and student-led events throughout the academic year.

With inter-house competitions, annual sports activities, athletics, recreational events, and college-level teams, NSC encourages students to participate, compete, and represent Nalapad with pride. The club aims to build a strong sporting culture that promotes discipline, teamwork, leadership, fitness, and a healthy spirit of competition.
