Our Story

Style as a form
of self-expression.

The Nalapad Fashion Club was born from a belief that creativity and healthcare are not opposites — they are deeply intertwined. Our nurses are artists of care, and the Fashion Club gives them a stage to celebrate identity, heritage, and imagination.

From our signature Milan Cultural Ceremony to national-level runway competitions, the Club has earned a reputation for bold storytelling, meticulous design, and breathtaking stage presence.
