Our Story

Culture, Creativity
and Celebration.

Milan – NGI's Festive Fusion is Nalapad's signature cultural celebration, bringing together students through music, fashion, dance, art, and entertainment. The festival provides a vibrant platform for students to showcase their creativity and talent while creating memorable experiences across the campus.

Featured highlights include Navarasa '25 — an inter-college fashion show, live performances from Hananshah and Sooraj Santhosh, a DJ & entertainment experience with DJ Olaras, plus multiple stage events and student competitions throughout the festival.
