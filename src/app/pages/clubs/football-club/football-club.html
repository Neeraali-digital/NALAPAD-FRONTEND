Our Story

A brotherhood
forged on the pitch.

For more than two decades, the Nalapad Football Club has been at the heart of student life — uniting nursing students from across our campuses through the beautiful game. From early-morning training sessions to last-minute winners in front of roaring crowds, the Club is more than a team — it's a family.

Today, we field competitive squads across men's and women's divisions, regularly representing Karnataka in regional and national tournaments.
