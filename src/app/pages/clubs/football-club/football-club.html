Our Story

A Culture of Competition.
A Legacy of Excellence.

Established in 2025, the Nalapad Football Championship is an inter-college football tournament that brings together teams from colleges and institutions, as well as eligible teams from outside the campus. Conducted under the guidance of KSFA, the championship aims to create a competitive platform where emerging football talent can compete at a high level.

The tournament's Ever-Rolling Trophy represents more than victory — it is a symbol of competition, achievement, and the legacy built by every year's champions.
