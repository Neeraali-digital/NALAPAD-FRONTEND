Our Story

Quality Nursing Education, Built for Real Careers

Nalapad Group of Institutions was established in 2021 under the Nalapad Health and Educational Trust, led by Chairman Sri N. A. Haris, with a vision to provide quality higher and professional education.

At Nalapad College of Nursing, strong academic foundations are combined with practical clinical exposure and industry-relevant skills. Students train in specialised labs and at our parent hospitals, building the confidence to deliver patient care from day one.

Beyond academics, students take part in clubs, houses, cultural events and leadership programmes that develop communication, teamwork and professional skills.
